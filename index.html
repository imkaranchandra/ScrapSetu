Our Idea

ScrapSetu aims to close the loop between waste generators, collectors, and recyclers. By providing a digital marketplace, we enable:

  • Collectors to register scrap items quickly and get fair price estimates.
  • Recyclers to view incoming lots, schedule pickups and manage their inventory.
  • Transparent pricing based on current market rates for different materials.
  • Easy analytics for both parties to track volume, earnings and environmental impact.
