Who We Are

ScrapSetu is a digital platform that connects scrap collectors with recyclers, making the recycling process simpler, more transparent, and organized.

We aim to create a digital bridge between people who collect recyclable materials and businesses that process and recycle them. ScrapSetu helps collectors find better opportunities while allowing recyclers to discover available scrap and manage their collection process efficiently.

Our platform focuses on fair pricing, easy connections, digital tracking, and responsible waste management . We believe technology can make recycling more accessible while supporting the people who play an important role in keeping our communities cleaner.

Our Mission

Our mission is to build a smarter recycling ecosystem where collectors, recyclers, and recyclable materials are connected through one simple digital platform.

What We Do

  • Connect Collectors & Recyclers – Bringing both sides together on one platform.
  • Support Fair Pricing – Helping users understand current scrap rates.
  • Simplify Scrap Management – Making listings, pickups, and transactions easier.
  • Enable Digital Tracking – Helping users track their activities and recycling impact.
  • Promote Sustainable Recycling – Supporting a cleaner and more organized recycling ecosystem.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I sign up? Click the "Collector Portal" or "Recycler Portal" button on the Home tab and use the sign-up form.
  • Is there any cost? Registration is free. A small commission is taken on completed transactions.
  • What materials are accepted? Plastic, paper, metal such as iron, copper, aluminium, steel, and e-waste.
  • How is pricing determined? Prices are based on the scrap rates displayed on the platform.
