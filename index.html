About ScrapSetu

Building India's Smartest Circular Waste Highway

ScrapSetu is a transformative digital ecosystem created to bridge the deep chasm between grassroots scrap collectors and modern industrial processing units.

For decades, India's waste management and scrap trading sector has remained heavily fragmented, opaque, and controlled by informal middlemen. ScrapSetu equips informal waste workers with technological dignity, fair real-time rates, and structured access to institutional buyers.

Help & Clarity

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining ScrapSetu as a collector, aggregator, or recycling plant.

Creating an account is quick and free. Click on either the Collector Portal or Recycler Portal buttons on the home tab, then choose "Sign Up". Provide your mobile number, verify via OTP, choose your city, and your digital scrap ID is instantly activated.
No. Registration and account maintenance are 100% free for both scrap collectors and recycling facilities. ScrapSetu operates on a nominal micro-transaction fee deducted only when a verified trade is successfully settled.
We accept all major categories: ferrous metals (Iron, Steel, Cast Iron), non-ferrous metals (Copper, Aluminium, Brass, Lead, Zinc), plastics (HDPE, LDPE, PET bottles, PP), paper (Old corrugated cardboard, newspaper, office files), and lead-acid battery scrap.
Prices are algorithmically indexed against regional mandi trading benchmarks, MCX commodity rates, and verified industrial smelter purchase contracts. Rates are refreshed daily to ensure fair and transparent remuneration.
All weigh-ins take place on calibrated digital scales or certified weighbridges. A digital receipt with time, location, and weight is generated automatically. Payouts are dispatched immediately to the collector's registered UPI ID or bank account.
